All pixel scenery and characters are drawn in scripts/main.gd.
Bandit variations are generated and drawn in scripts/bandit_appearance.gd.
The four civilian designs are drawn in scripts/civilian_appearance.gd.
Procedural city layouts are created in scripts/street_generator.gd.
The four PCM sound effects are synthesized by scripts/generate_audio.py.
The short original English lines are synthesized with eSpeak NG (en-us).
No music, film audio, third-party images or downloaded game assets are included.

To regenerate: install espeak-ng, then run python3 scripts/generate_audio.py.
Speech engine: https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng
